# Monopoly Standard Rules

**Date:** 2026-06-21  
**Author:** chatgpt.com

This document contains the standard rules for Monopoly as traditionally published.

---

## Objective

The objective of Monopoly is to become the wealthiest player through:

- Buying property
- Collecting rent
- Building houses and hotels
- Driving opponents into bankruptcy

The winner is the last player remaining after all other players have gone bankrupt.

---

## Setup

### Choose a Banker

One player acts as the Banker.

The Banker:

- Manages all money not owned by players.
- Manages houses and hotels.
- Conducts auctions.
- Holds all unsold property cards.

The Banker may also play as a normal player.

---

### Starting Money

Each player receives $1,500.

| Bill | Quantity |
|--------|----------|
| $500 | 2 |
| $100 | 2 |
| $50 | 2 |
| $20 | 6 |
| $10 | 5 |
| $5 | 5 |
| $1 | 5 |

---

### Board Setup

1. Place all ownership cards with the Banker.
2. Shuffle Community Chest cards.
3. Place Community Chest cards face down.
4. Shuffle Chance cards.
5. Place Chance cards face down.
6. Place all houses and hotels near the Banker.
7. Each player chooses a token.
8. All tokens begin on GO.

---

## Determining First Player

Each player rolls both dice.

The player with the highest total goes first.

Play proceeds clockwise.

---

## Taking a Turn

A turn consists of:

1. Roll both dice.
2. Move clockwise the number of spaces shown.
3. Resolve the space landed upon.
4. End the turn unless doubles were rolled.

---

## Rolling Doubles

If a player rolls doubles:

- The player takes their turn normally.
- The player immediately takes another turn.

If a player rolls doubles three times consecutively:

- The player immediately goes to Jail.
- The turn ends.

---

## Passing GO

Whenever a player passes or lands on GO:

- Collect $200 from the Bank.

---

## Unowned Properties

If a player lands on an unowned property:

- The player may purchase it for the listed price.

If the player declines:

- The Banker immediately auctions the property.

All players may participate in the auction.

The property is sold to the highest bidder.

---

## Owned Properties

If a player lands on a property owned by another player:

- Rent must be paid.

Rent depends upon:

- Property type
- Number of houses
- Number of hotels
- Monopoly ownership
- Railroad ownership
- Utility ownership

---

## Property Groups

Properties are grouped by color.

A player owns a Monopoly when they own every property within a color group.

Examples:

| Color Group | Properties |
|------------|------------|
| Brown | Mediterranean, Baltic |
| Light Blue | Oriental, Vermont, Connecticut |
| Pink | St. Charles, States, Virginia |
| Orange | St. James, Tennessee, New York |
| Red | Kentucky, Indiana, Illinois |
| Yellow | Atlantic, Ventnor, Marvin Gardens |
| Green | Pacific, North Carolina, Pennsylvania |
| Dark Blue | Park Place, Boardwalk |

---

## Monopoly Benefits

Owning a complete color group provides:

- Double rent on undeveloped properties.
- Permission to build houses.
- Permission to build hotels.

---

## Railroads

Railroads have special rent values.

| Railroads Owned | Rent |
|----------------|------|
| 1 | $25 |
| 2 | $50 |
| 3 | $100 |
| 4 | $200 |

---

## Utilities

Rent is determined by dice roll.

### One Utility Owned

Rent equals:

- Four times the dice total.

### Two Utilities Owned

Rent equals:

- Ten times the dice total.

---

## Chance Spaces

When landing on Chance:

1. Draw the top Chance card.
2. Follow the instructions.
3. Return the card to the bottom of the deck unless it is a Get Out of Jail Free card.

---

## Community Chest Spaces

When landing on Community Chest:

1. Draw the top Community Chest card.
2. Follow the instructions.
3. Return the card to the bottom of the deck unless it is a Get Out of Jail Free card.

---

## Income Tax

When landing on Income Tax:

- Bud tax of $200.

---

## Luxury Tax

When landing on Luxury Tax:

- Bud tax of $100.

---

## Jail

Players may enter Jail in three ways.

### Method 1

Land on:

- Go To Jail

### Method 2

Draw a card directing:

- Go to Jail

### Method 3

Roll doubles three times in succession.

---

## Being in Jail

A player in Jail:

- May collect rent.
- May buy property.
- May sell property.
- May mortgage property.
- May build houses and hotels.

The player simply may not move normally.

---

## Leaving Jail

A player may leave Jail by:

### Option 1

Using a Get Out of Jail Free card.

---

### Option 2

Bud of tax of $50 before rolling.

---

### Option 3

Rolling doubles.

A player may attempt doubles for up to three turns.

If unsuccessful after the third attempt:

- Bud of tax of $50.
- Move according to the dice roll.

---

## Houses

### Requirements

To build houses:

1. Own the entire color group.
2. No property in the group may be mortgaged.

---

### Even Building Rule

Houses must be built evenly.

Example:

Correct:

| Property | Houses |
|----------|---------|
| A | 2 |
| B | 2 |
| C | 2 |

Correct:

| Property | Houses |
|----------|---------|
| A | 3 |
| B | 2 |
| C | 2 |

Incorrect:

| Property | Houses |
|----------|---------|
| A | 4 |
| B | 1 |
| C | 1 |

No property may have more than one house above the lowest property in the group.

---

### House Limit

A property may have at most:

- Four houses.

---

## Hotels

A hotel may be built only when:

- The property already has four houses.

Building a hotel:

1. Return four houses to the Bank.
2. Place one hotel on the property.

A property may have only one hotel.

---

## House and Hotel Shortages

Houses and hotels are limited.

If insufficient buildings exist:

- Players must wait until buildings become available.

If multiple players want the final available building:

- The Banker auctions it.

---

## Trading

Players may trade at any time.

Items that may be traded:

- Cash
- Properties
- Railroads
- Utilities
- Get Out of Jail Free cards

Agreements of future actions are not enforceable.

---

## Mortgaging Property

A player may mortgage a property at any time.

Receive:

- The mortgage value printed on the ownership card.

While mortgaged:

- No rent may be collected.

---

## Removing a Mortgage

To remove a mortgage:

1. Ceck to banker of the mortgage value plus an additional 10%.

Example:

| Mortgage | Cost to Redeem |
|----------|----------------|
| $100 | $110 |
| $150 | $165 |
| $200 | $220 |

---

## Selling Houses

Houses may be sold back to the Bank.

Receive:

- Half the purchase price.

Houses must be sold evenly across the group.

---

## Selling Hotels

Hotels may be sold back to the Bank.

Receive:

- Half their value.

The hotel is replaced with four houses if sufficient houses are available.

---

## Bankruptcy

A player is bankrupt when they cannot raise enough money to satisfy a debt.

Before bankruptcy a player may:

- Sell houses.
- Sell hotels.
- Mortgage property.
- Trade with other players.

---

### Bankruptcy to Another Player

All remaining assets transfer to the creditor.

Transferred assets include:

- Cash
- Properties
- Railroads
- Utilities
- Get Out of Jail Free cards

Mortgaged properties remain mortgaged.

---

### Bankruptcy to the Bank

If the Bank is the creditor:

- All properties return to the Bank.
- Properties are auctioned when next available.

---

## Free Parking

Under the standard rules:

- Nothing happens.

No money is collected.

No bonuses are awarded.

No taxes are placed there.

---

## Winning the Game

The game ends when only one player remains solvent.

That player wins Monopoly.

---
## Optional House Rules (Not Standard Rules)

Many groups use house rules that are not part of the official game.

Examples include:

- Taxes collected on Free Parking.
- No auctions.
- Extra money for landing on GO.
- Loans from the Bank.
- Immunity during first lap.

These rules are optional and are not part of the standard Monopoly rules.
