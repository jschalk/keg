# Farm Dream

**Date:** 2026-08-27  
**Author:** Emmanuel Schalk   
**Source:** Dream, as best I can remember it   

---

*I had a dream last night that I was on a farm that had recently been visited by army irregulars who were not friendly. A male in our family was killed. The irregulars left and I was in front of dozens of faces looking at me for answers. After some of the boys yelled they wanted to join an army and kill for revenge. I pulled the most mature boy over.*

<div align="center">

### So you want to be a Man?
</div>

Makes sense after what happened this morning. That'll end your childhood right quick. You want to get a gun and join an army? Which army? None of them will bring Louis back. 

So you want to be a man? Ok then I won't have to ask you. 

Today we're in this together. I'm leading right now but tomorrow it could be you. You need to learn everything and be willing to do it. And an army of a fever could take me over the winder and then it'll be you, your aunts and maybe William in case he decides to be a man. 

Water, Foot, Shelter. We're in a race now. Winter is coming and the armies are coming and when the armies are cold, hungry they forage. You know that word? A general sits in a warm tent drinking wine by a fine fire and hears "the men need supplies". The general says "Send out foraging parties" Then straving half naked bands of 20 men come to this farm to take everything they can. They eat the food, tear down the house for firewood, rape the women and the boys and us if it interests them. Take us away to be irregulars in their army. If we're lucky they'll take only me. 

Here's what we're going to do - We're going to get the rest of the family through this. This winter and the next until the warring ends. Anna, think of Anna, she's already reading and she's only 5. We're going to get her through this and she will not only be alive, but thriving curious and happy. 

Water - We've got two sources: the well next to the house and the stream. During war armies will throw dead animals or men into wells to poison them. We need to widen the house well so that one of us can go down and get any carcasses out. We'll need a rope ladder and strong bucket rope. We'll dig out a dry cave hole in the south side of little hill to store them in. It's pretty far from the well but it's only going to be used when carcasses need to be removed. 

Lastly we're going to have to build a secret well in Mary's forest. It's rocky but we'll find a spot near the meadow. Which we won't farm on anymore.

Food - We're going to make a much food as we can. We're going to stash it everywhere we can. We're going to hide that we have food by being really skinny. All of us. And very pale. No more sunburns. We work at night under moonlight whenever we can. 

Men only still from the wealthy, we're going to look like poor drages living off cabbage and roots. Not worth their time. 

The second well and it's food stashes will be hidden, that means no paths. We'll build a staircase to go up gilberts rocks aand we'll and we'll take it down when winter comes. After that we will climb the rocks every time. 

The house will be gone and broken by the end of winter. We're going to make it look like it's already been ransacked. It could have been this morning. We'll make hovels next to the house and live there with everyone. We'll have backup hovels in Mary's forest, we'll use wood from the house to build those. 

This will be a lot of work. And we need to do it on little food. You're not Casey anymore. You're Casefield. Don't let anyone call you your old name. 

You'll need a wife. You're only 14 but you'll need a wife for all the trails ahead. Probably Iona, she's older than you and she's not a fool. We'll talk to her father. 

You'll need to get close to God. Ask Him for guidance and protection. I am a wise, logical, learned man. Wisdom has been my obsession my whole life. But we will make mistakes. Those we love will die. Without God we will not have strength. I'm going to give you everything I have. But you'll need God. If I can point you to him you will save this family. Even if I die. You will.
