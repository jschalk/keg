### My Philosophical Background

My philosophical father is Jules Simon, I took many classes with him at UTEP. He introduced me to Levinas, Husserl, Heidegger, Arendt, Ricoeur and of course Simon (his work). Others too. Most of these Philosophers can be described as Phenomenologists. This comes from the Husserl being the big philosopher and using the word 'Phenomenology' to describe his work. Another famous philosopher named Hegel wrote a book many decades Husserl called a 'Phenomenology of Spirit" so the word has been around.

## Naming this project
 
Here I have my project. I want it to be accessible to a general audience. I want a short, understandable name. The name I could become more important then my name. 

For example with Husserl he was the big guy in Phenomenology mainly because he was the first guy. Other smarter people took up the ideas and did more with them. Heidegger, Levinas, Rosenzweig, Ricouer, Arendt were all better philosophers then Husserl. There are philosophers in the world who study Phenomenology and never read Husserl. This is perfectly legitimate to me becuase it's impossible to read everything and the other thinkers are awesome. 

I am the first on this project, which is a sub-project of Emmanuel Levinas's Ethics as First Philosophy, which is a sub-project of the Phenomenology project. If this project becomes important my contribution will be buried under the work of greater minds. It is likely. All the people I've listed above were identified by their classmates and teachers as exceptionally brilliant. I was only ever identified and "smart but lazy", "too much of a smartass". There are no metrics that support me being a seminal thinker on the level of this project. Maybe I got lucky, maybe I found something, but if I did, others will come and take the reigns. That's the best case scenario. 

If that's the best case scenario then I better choose a good name. 

## Where Kegology comes from. 

I was searching for synonyms for containers, which I do often because when programming there are things that hold other things all the time. 

These things need distinct names so I can understand what's going on. The shorter the name the better but also it is really helpful if the order of the letters in the name does not appear anywhere else in the code. This way doing a 'find and replace' later can avoid breaking the code. 

For example 'sue' and 'issue' overlap so if I do find and replace of 'sue' with 'bob' it would make 'issue' into  'isbob' which is unreadable. 

Given all this I was searching for synonyms for containers and somehow 'keg' came up. I checked and 'keg' did not appear anywhere in the hundreds of files or tens of thousands of lines of code. That was pretty cool. 

In my personal experience kegs are metal containers that keep beer under pressure. I knew no other definition. Checking online I found the same. (Here's one I found today: ['a small cask or barrel having a capacity of 30 gallons or less'](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/keg)) 

So then I had a decision to make: do I use 'keg'? I went back to my philosophical training: words have the definition we give them. The definition of a word can change. It can mean different things to different people.

During my formative years I went to El Paso High School in El Paso, Texas. The internet had just come out but did not dominate like it does now. There were no cell phones. My friends and schoolmates often met socially to have parties. Organizing parties was an organic process, there was no central committee in charge. So any person could decide to have a party. They would need to convince their friends, get the word around. Naturally this meant there was a competition of parties. Sometimes parties would be organized and many people would come and sometimes nobody would come. The parties that were serious got more people. What as the number one way to prove you were serious? Get a keg. 

If a party had a keg or two that meant the party was serious and many people would go. It would be fun. There would be enough beer for everyone. So in a way kegs united a free people. Everyone was free to pick their party and would never go to a party where they were disrespected. The keg symbolized a scarifice of the party host ala I will give much money for everyone else's enjoyment. 

If there was a keg and I knew someone was sacrificing for me and my friends. And people did this all the time. 

I think about this sometimes when I write *Kegology*.
