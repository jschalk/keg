## Tubes

Renoir (the father) declares that tubes are the reason Paris lost so much of it's personality. *TODO: add quote from book*

So before the tubes brought everything to the properties really nice places in Paris were nice because they were so limited. There were only 5 wells in Montmarte so only 5 wells of people could live there. But now everyone gets a large apartment, a house with a toliet and no effort required to get rid of poop. 

Currently there is a conflict between Ukraine and Russia that is destorying 100s of thousands of lives. Even those not mained physically will bear war trauma scars for the next 50 years. And yet there is a tube that functions perfectly well from Russia through Ukraine on to the rest of Europe. Russia could destory it but doesn't. Ukraine could destory it but doesn't. I don't know how the agreement works, who pays whom for what but since Russia is the bigger guy Russia probably pays transit fees to Ukraine and then sells gas to Germany. The arrangement works for both sides. The gas probably makes it all the way to Spain. So the gas moves through tubes and every country along the way gets paid. 

It's different from the neighborhood gas. There is a gas utility. They use public land to bury the gas tubes. There is one tube connection per house. The gas travels from the gas company property through the public gas tubes to your house. There are no transit fees. Imagine instead that you gas came from a tube connected to your neighbor's house. Then your neighbor would get a transit fee. And their neighbor. All the way to the source. The transit fees would be minicule. Back in the age of paper that kind of accounting would be impossible. With computers it's easy. 

The other cool thing about having gas come from your neighbor is that you could have gas pipes come from each of your touching neighbors. If that was the case you could buy gas from the cheapest source, say between 2 neighbors, and then sell to two other neighbors. Perhaps sometime buy gas from neighbor A and sometimes you sell to neighbor A. If your house was the first house in a row of houses up a road you'd need enough piping the gas of all the rest. But if you charged too much they'd just make their own pipe going around your property. There's challenges sure but the main thing is that instead of a faceless entity as the source of your gas there would be a face. You would know who your gas comes from.
