*Am I forgiven if I plan to become a celebrity?*

---

This article is not so much a logical argument as a rant. It is perfectly legitimate to ignore. There are probably logical contradictions.

## Norman the Doorman

A TV show in the United States once had a short comedy sketch where three people on a game show had to remember the names of celebrities and people in their life[^1]. The joke was that the contestants could easily remember 3rd and 4th tier celebrities but couldn't remember the people in their 'meatspace' lives. One of them even had a name that rhymed. 'Norman the Doorman'. 

This sketch quickly and efficiently reminds me of one of my great shames: 

<div align="center">

### I know so much more about celebrities then I know about my neighbors. 
</div>

I learn a lot about celebrities from my doomscrolling. 

I doomscroll a lot, it should be a diagnosible condition but the authors of the Diagnostics and Statistics Manual must be in league with the Tech giants because there is none. I have "YouTube Shorts Addiction" 

<div align="center">

### Seriously, fuck you Tech Firms.
</div>

They should all be nationalized. 

---

Sorry for the aside. Here's the important thing: Not knowing anything about the neighbors is exactly what makes a population so pliable. Don't make friends, don't have alliances, join together for political action and so forth. 

In a fatalistic sense it makes sense not to bother learning your neighbor's names. They have no real influence. Even if neighbors get together and support a political cause they'll never be invited into power. The big example for me is the 2007 Democratic primary election campaign. Obama and Hillary were in a tight race and both engaged 10 of thousands, maybe 100s of thousands of volunteers. Who were immediately forgotten after the election and not given any hand in governing.   

---

There are essential two places to put your faith. In the people around you or in the people far away. The people far away are powerful but busy and not good at listening. The people nearby aren't powerful and also are busy and not good at listening. 

So why bother going to the neighbors. Better to just know what the kings are doing. 

## Need to Dive into Neighborhood

I intuitively believe that if good things are going to happen it will be because neighborhoods got stronger. More influence. So I need to know my neighbors' names.

And since there are limits to my ability to remember names: I need to choose to remember my neighbors over celebrities. It's hard to do because my neighbors are people and celebrities have whole regiments of professionals working to make them interesting. My neighbors don't. 

But each new celebrity means one less neighbor. 

And I need to complete the Introduction to Kegology first. Which keeps me alone and in front of the computer. 

## Need to complete Intro and present it

It also presents a terrible conclusion: 

<div align="center">

### Presenting Kegology means I'm asking to become a celebrity. 
</div>

I'm going to be part of the very system I claim is hurting my family and friends.

Instead of accepting that implication here's my promise: I will not promote other celebrities for they sake of their celebrity.

And I will plan that myself and others reduce the celebrities that we know. Get off the internet and touch grass. Gossip about celebrities all you want, just try to make it only the neighbors who know whats going on.

And maybe you'll forgive me.
