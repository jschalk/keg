## Assumed Knowledge

If you're reading this the assumption is that you've decided that you want to keg-jump. One part of that jump is to create a currency for your MomentUnit that is useful to others. To be useful they have to understand it and believe in it. Which means something pretty scary:

<div align="center">

#### You have to learn how money supply works.
</div>

Using money is one thing most everyone knows how do. Creating/managing a money supply and giving it value is something very few know how to do. There is an overwhelming number of books about monetary policy. Read any that are helpful, or ask an AI to give you an explanation. Here I'll lay out my suggestion for the best way to learn how to create a use a money supply. 

<div align="center">

#### Play a game of Monopoly
</div>

Play Monopoly and give attention to the money supply. The Game of Monopoly is great. It's clean simple fun. It shows how monopolies work. People have been playing for almost 100 years. Generations have played it. And it's a common accepted practice that household set their own rules their way. If you've played Monopoly before just do everything like you did before. If it's your first game just get the [game pieces](monopoly_parts.md) and follow the [rules](monopoly_rules.md) loosely and have fun. 

<div align="center">

#### Here's the discipline: track the money supply. 
</div>

Use paper or a spreadsheet and create your own accounting system that answers these questions:
- How much cash is there in the game? ($15,140)
- How much cash is in play (the money supply)?
- What is printed value of all the properties? ($5,600)
- What is predicted aution-value of all the properties? ($5,600?) *Make your own system that predicts how much each property would get at aution*

Watch how the game changes given the cash in play. What happens when there is very little cash in play? What happens when there is a lot of money at play? Every time a player passes Go the money supply increases. What happens to the values of properties? They should go up? Isn't that interesting: the price of the property changes even if the property hasn't changed at all.

<div align="center">

#### What a thing is doesn't decide it's price.<br><br> The market decides the price and the money supply changes the market. 
</div>

Change the rules your way so that you can learn new things about a money supply. Can you change the rules so there is a real estate bubble?

Here's where it gets fun. 
<div align="center">

#### Can the monopoly money be used to make a player do something outside the game? 
</div>


Before a game create a set of coupons such as:
- This coupon is good for the first piece of cake when there is cake.
- This coupon is good for the first use of bathroom between 7am and 8am.
- This coupon is good for one cold water from Sue.
- This coupon is good for the extra pizza slice if multiple people want it.

During the game auction off the coupons. Predict how much they will sell for. Predict how much they will sell for later in the game. Can you create a coupon bubble where prices go up and that get overvalued until prices drop?

- Consider adding a timer to the coupon, must be used within the game. 
- Create a banking council of elected players. Have the banking council give orders to the banker.
- Consider making the game cyclical instead of ending with one player.  If the banking council decides it can procecute a player for having a monopoly and fine the player for all of their assets in the monopoly. The properties can go back on the auction block. Then winner is whoever has the most assets after each player has taken 10 turns. The game can be stopped for the day and started again later.  


## Other possible questions

Here are some other questions that can be considered:
- What is the predicted aution-value of each of the 16 community cards?
- What is the predicted aution-value of each of the 16 chance cards?
- If a random community card was auctioned how much would it sell for?
- If a random chance card was auctioned how much would it sell for?


## Conclusion

Kegology makes it possible to create and manage a currency with only Excel skills. To make your currency useful to others and thus to yourself requires discipline. With it others will have confidence.
